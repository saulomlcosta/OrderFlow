What to observe while testing

Create and stock

Reserve first

Starting checkout reduces available stock without creating an order yet.

Cancel safely

Cancellation releases reserved quantities and makes them available again.

Complete atomically

Completion confirms stock deduction and creates the order snapshot in one business step.
